Welcome to the official **Arsenal** documentation. Arsenal (*or HD2 Arsenal*) is a desktop companion for **Helldivers 2**: a mod manager, a Home hub for the galactic war, and a dedicated lobby system for playing together with the same mods.

## What is Arsenal?

Arsenal is built around three areas you open from the sidebar:

| Area | What it is for |
|------|----------------|
| **Home** | Featured mods chosen by the Arsenal staff, Helldivers 2 news, community videos, and live war status |
| **Mod Management** | Library, profiles, import, options, deploy, and launch |
| **Lobby** | Find or create rooms, sync a mod loadout, and drop in with other Helldivers |

You can also package mods in the [Mod Builder](/mod-builder) (0.36 and later). Docs for **0.30.2 and earlier** are under **Legacy** in the sidebar version menu.

## Home

Home is the first screen after onboarding. It pulls live data from Arsenal and from Helldivers 2.

### Featured Mods

A rotating showcase of mods selected by the Arsenal staff. Each entry shows artwork, title, author, and a short description. Click the main card to open the linked page (usually Nexus Mods). Thumbnails and dots let you jump between mods.

### War status

The right panel tracks the galactic war.

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **Top Planets** | Active campaigns (liberation, defense, reclamation). Progress rates, HP, regions, and faction control. Click a planet for description, environmentals, statistics, and subfactions. |
| **Major Orders** | Current Super Earth orders: overview, task progress, rewards, and time remaining. Arsenal can also notify you when a new Major Order is issued. |

### News and videos

The lower panel has two tabs:

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **Latest News** | Helldivers 2 news (updates, events, and general news). Open an item for the full article, with optional Steam and video links. |
| **Community Videos** | Curated Helldivers 2 videos. Click a card to watch it in Arsenal. |

## Lobby

Open **[Lobby](/lobby)** from the sidebar (people icon). Sign in with **Discord** to browse rooms, create a lobby, sync a mod loadout, and use friends and clans. The full walkthrough is in [Lobby System](/lobby).

## Quick Start

The in-app Mod Management tutorial is documented as a full walkthrough under [Quick Start](/quick-start). You can replay it anytime from **Settings > Mod Management > Replay Tutorial**.

1. Download and install [Arsenal](https://rsnl.gg/downloads)
2. Complete the onboarding wizard
3. Follow [Quick Start](/quick-start) (or the in-app tour) to import, enable, deploy, and launch
